[Intro — spoken over the groove]
Well, Moab had been collecting rent for eighteen years.
Then Ehud came to pay a visit.

[Verse 1]
King Eglon had a summer room, a throne, a fancy door,
And folks lined up with tribute like they always had before.
Ehud brought the payment, gave the guards a friendly grin,
With a double-edged little secret riding on his right-hand hip.
He sent the other fellows home, then turned around once more:
"I got a private word for His Majesty,
If he'll kindly clear the floor."

[Verse 2]
Out went every servant. Up got Eglon from his seat.
"I've got a message from God," said Ehud, soft and sweet.
His left hand found the dagger. Well, the message landed hard.
Then he slipped out past the porch, locked the door, and left the guard.

[Chorus]
Don't knock, he's busy! Give the king a little time.
That's what the palace boys kept saying
While Ehud made the quarry line.
Don't knock, he's busy! Ain't it nice to be polite?
They gave him every minute that he needed
To get clean out of sight.

[Verse 3]
One guard said, "He's relieving himself."
The others said, "Fair enough."
Nobody wanted to be the one
To interrupt that royal stuff.
They waited till it got embarrassing,
Then somebody fetched the key.
The king was on the floor by then,
And Ehud was running free.

[Chorus]
Don't knock, he's busy! Give the king a little time.
That's what the palace boys kept saying
While Ehud made the quarry line.
Don't knock, he's busy! Ain't it nice to be polite?
They gave him every minute that he needed
To get clean out of sight.

[Bridge]
Ehud blew the horn on Ephraim's hill,
And Israel came down fast.
They held the fords against Moab,
And the king's long reign was past.
The land got eighty quiet years;
That's how the chapter tells it.
But down at Eglon's palace, I'll bet
They still check the door themselves.

[Final chorus]
Don't knock, he's busy! Give the king a little time.
That's what the palace boys kept saying
While Ehud made the quarry line.
Don't knock, he's busy! That was their mistake:
They minded all the king's good manners
While Ehud made his break.

[Tag — spoken]
Next time the king's got a secret meeting,
Maybe knock anyway.
